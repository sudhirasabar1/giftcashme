Frequently Asked Questions

Is GiftCash really free?

Yes, joining is free. You complete sponsored offers from our partners, and they pay us a commission. We share that revenue with you in the form of reward points. You never pay us anything.

How long does it take to earn a gift card?

Most members complete 2–3 offers in 5–15 minutes. The exact time depends on the offers available in your region.

Do I need to provide payment info?

Some offers may ask for a credit card for a free trial. Those are optional — you can choose offers that don't require payment info.

When will I receive my gift card?

Once you hit the points threshold, you can redeem instantly. Gift cards are delivered to your email within 24–48 hours.

Is my personal information safe?

We only collect the email needed for reward delivery. Offer partners have their own privacy policies.
